CLIENT PHOTOS & VIDEOS
======================

photos/   web-optimised WebP photos (max 1200px)
video/    <name>-loop.mp4   short, muted, sped-up loop that autoplays on the page
          <name>-full.mp4   full clip with sound (opens in the pop-up player)
          <name>-poster.webp still frame shown before a video loads

To add or swap media, put files here and edit the lists in
src/config/siteConfig.js  (services[].image, videos[], gallery[]).
Paths there are relative to /public, e.g. 'media/photos/my-photo.webp'.

Tips: photos as JPG/WebP ~1200px wide; videos as MP4 (H.264).
